Project Overview

One place to find everything analytics

Before Periscope, dashboards, reports, AI models, data extracts, and documentation were scattered across Power BITableauSignal 1VizientEpicSharePoint and internal apps. The goal was to create a centralized experience that improved discoverability, transparency, governance, and trust.

The Problem

Discovery was fragmented and trust was low

Analytics assets were fragmented across multiple systems, making discovery slow, inconsistent, and difficult to trust.

  • Users struggled to find trusted dashboards, reports, and AI models
  • Limited visibility into ownership and certification status
  • Duplicate reports created confusion and redundant work
  • Inconsistent metadata and documentation standards
  • No centralized way to discover assets, initiatives, or priorities
  • New employees faced a steep learning curve finding analytics resources
The Solution

A governed, Amazon-like discovery experience

We designed Periscope as a centralized single source of truth for analytics assets, AI models, KPIs, and initiatives.

  • Unified search and filters to quickly discover relevant assets
  • Asset detail pages with previews, owners, documentation, and access paths
  • Governance indicators for certification, metadata, and lifecycle status
  • Connected assets across Power BI, Tableau, Epic, AI platforms, and internal tools
  • Enabled users to save favorites, request access, and quickly launch assets
  • Campaigns grouped assets around priorities like Cost Reduction and HIMSS Stage 7

Phase 1 Criteria

User demand, business impact, governance value, technical feasibility, and ability to reduce duplicate work.

Through affinity mapping, dot-voting, and stakeholder workshops, we identified pain points, translated them into feature opportunities, and prioritized Phase 1.

Search was the primary need

Users knew what they wanted — they just didn't know where it lived.

Trust signals mattered

Ownership, certification, and metadata needed to be visible before launch.

Preview before launch

Users needed screenshots and context before opening reports.

Initiatives matter

Teams think in goals — Cost Reduction, Leapfrog, Quality Improvement.

Governance can't add friction

Governance needed to build trust without slowing users down.

Role-based discovery

Users wanted favorites, personalized recommendations, and popular-with-my-role signals.

Certification Status Framework

Six states, one source of truth

Each asset in the marketplace carries one of six governance states — visible to users before they open anything.

Certified

Trusted and approved for enterprise use.

In Review

Currently undergoing governance review.

Remediation Required

Updates needed before approval.

Not Eligible

Does not meet governance criteria.

Deprecated

No longer recommended for use.

Flagged

Requires review or justification.

Reflection

The hardest UX problem

This project combined enterprise UX, healthcare analytics, information architecture, AI governance, design systems, and data governance into one platform. The deepest challenge was not making assets easier to find — it was making users feel confident that what they found was trusted, governed, and appropriate for decision-making.
