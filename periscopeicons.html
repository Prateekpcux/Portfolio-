MY WORK ABOUT PLAY
BACKGROUND
Periscope is an Enterprise Analytics Marketplace designed to provide a One-Stop Shop for discovering, accessing, and understanding analytics assets across Inova Health System.

Prior to Periscope, Dashboards, Reports, AI Models, Data Extracts, and Documentation were distributed across multiple platforms including Power BI, Tableau, Signal 1, Vizient, Epic, SharePoint, and internal applications.

The goal was to create a centralized experience that improved Discoverability, Transparency, Governance, and Trust while reducing duplicate work across teams.
THE PROBLEM


Analytics assets were fragmented across multiple systems, making discovery slow, inconsistent, and difficult to trust.

  • Users struggled to find trusted dashboards, reports, and AI models
  • Limited visibility into ownership and certification status
  • Duplicate reports created confusion and redundant work
  • Inconsistent metadata and documentation standards
  • No centralized way to discover assets, initiatives, and organizational priorities
  • Difficulty identifying which asset for operational and clinical decision-making
  • New employees faced a steep learning curve when searching for analytics resources
THE SOLUTION


We designed Periscope as a centralized single source of truth for analytics assets, AI models, KPIs, and initiatives.

  • Unified search and filters to quickly discover relevant assets.
  • Asset detail pages with previews, owners, documentation, and access paths.
  • Governance indicators for certification, metadata, and lifecycle status.
  • Connected assets across Power BI, Tableau, Epic, AI platforms, and internal tools
  • Enabled users to save favorites, request access, and quickly launch assets
  • Campaigns grouped assets around priorities like Cost Reduction, Quality Improvement, and HIMSS Stage 7.

KEY INSIGHTS
What we learned from the intake and working sessions

Search was the primary need

Users often knew what they wanted but did not know where it lived.

Trust signals mattered

Ownership, certification, and metadata needed to be visible before launch.

Preview before launch

Users needed screenshots, descriptions, and context before opening reports.

Initiatives matter

Teams think in terms of goals like Cost Reduction, Leapfrog, and Quality Improvement.

Governance should not slow users

Governance needed to build trust without adding friction.

Role-based discovery was an opportunity

Users wanted “popular with my role,” favorites, and personalized recommendations.

ORGANIZING ASSETS
Campaigns and Inova Care pathways

Campaigns

A Campaign represents a high-level organizational initiative or strategic goal used to group and track related analytics assets, reports, dashboards, and AI models across the enterprise.


Examples include Leapfrog, Cost Reduction, Quality Improvement, and Patient Experience.

Inova Care

Inova Care focuses on clinical care pathways and patient care initiatives. Unlike Campaigns, Inova Care pathways are predefined and aligned with established clinical programs.


Examples include Sepsis Management, Stroke Care, Heart Failure, and Cancer Care.
