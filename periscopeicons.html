The Problem

Discoverability was the symptom. Trust was the disease.

Before Periscope

5 platforms. Zero coherence.

  • Assets spread across Power BI, Tableau, Signal 1, Vizient, Epic, SharePoint
  • No shared taxonomy — duplicate reports everywhere
  • No certification visibility — users emailed team before trusting any asset
  • AI models with no governance, oversight, or expiry tracking
  • New employees had no onboarding path into analytics
High bounce off stale reports Duplicate work Low data trust
The Mandate

One governed marketplace. Every asset. Trusted.

  • Unified search + browse across all platforms and asset types
  • Certification state visible on every asset card — not buried in metadata
  • AI model inventory with oversight, performance, and approval chain
  • Campaigns for clinical users who think in outcomes, not taxonomies
  • Governance workflows for admins — certify, deprecate, manage lifecycle

Critical Decisions

Three calls that shaped the platform.

01

Governance visible at card level — not detail page

  • Early: certification buried in metadata tab
  • Users scrolled past it every time
  • Fix: elevated to card surface — status badge, owner, last validated
→ Trust at browse time
02

Campaigns — parallel browse for clinical users

  • Clinical users think in initiatives, not taxonomies
  • Taxonomy browse failed them in sessions
  • Fix: Campaign path groups assets by HIMSS, Cost Reduction, Heart Failure
→ 2× faster time-to-asset
03

AI models — first-class assets, not a tab

  • Initial scope: AI = one asset type filter
  • AI carries oversight obligations no other asset has
  • Fix: dedicated inventory with performance, expiry, bias review
→ Inova's first AI governance UX

Design System

Built on Inova's enterprise foundation.

Periscope was built entirely within Inova's enterprise design system — Arial typeface, semantic color tokens, and established component patterns. The design work wasn't about inventing new styles; it was about extending the system with patterns the marketplace needed that didn't exist yet.

Outcomes

Two numbers. Both earned.

68%
faster time-to-find an asset
How we measured it
  • →Timed users finding a specific certified asset in the old system (Power BI + SharePoint + Teams) vs Periscope
  • →Old system avg: 6.2 min. Periscope avg: 2.0 min across 3 rounds of moderated testing
  • →Driven by governance status visible on the card — users stopped second-guessing which asset to trust
20K+
active users at launch
How we got there
  • →Replaced 5+ disconnected platforms in a single Phase 1 deployment across Inova Health System
  • →Adoption driven by Campaigns path — clinical users found their audit and initiative assets without needing to learn a new taxonomy
  • →Zero training sessions required — the experience was intuitive enough that teams onboarded independently

Periscope transformed data governance from a back-office function into a discoverable, trusted, user-centered enterprise product. The deepest design challenge: making users feel confident that what they found was certified and appropriate for clinical decision-making.

Next Steps

What I'd Do Differently

  • Stakeholder input was strong — real users would've accelerated confidence
