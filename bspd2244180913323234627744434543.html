Background

The nation's 5th largest telecom, newly independent

Brightspeed is valued at over $7.5 billion USD and is expanding its fiber optics network to reach 1 million new connections by 2023. As a spin-off of Lumen and CenturyLink, it serves 6.5 million locations in rural and suburban areas across the southeast, southwest, and great lakes regions, focusing on fiber-based telecom solutions for B2C and B2B markets.

The Problem

Post-acquisition brand and UX fragmentation

In November 2021, Apollo Global Management struck a deal with Lumen Technologies to acquire Brightspeed. The website experience felt disjointed due to branding associated with Lumen and CenturyLink pages, subpar navigation, and degraded support pages. Existing users dropped off, leading to significant client attrition.

The Solution

A unified 2.0 B2B experience

CGI's HCx team was brought on to oversee the Brightspeed client relationship and assist in migrating "cloned pages" from Lumen. Beyond migration, we redesigned their landing pages and product pages in Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) to increase customer acquisition while reducing attrition through improved CX.

My Role & Roadmap

Senior Product Designer -leading migration and redesign

I joined the team as Senior Product Designer and worked alongside a UX writer, UX strategist, and supervised two UX designers for the entire migration effort. We had two main goals spanning close to a year of work.

What we learned from workshops and stakeholder interviews

Through user interviews and workshops with our stakeholders, we identified the core gaps and opportunities for the new experience.

Navigation clarity

Trust through branding

Lingering Lumen/CenturyLink branding eroded trust. A consistent Brightspeed visual identity was critical.

Working Sessions & 15 Scrum Sprints

Iterative delivery across 15 two-week sprints

We prioritized redesigning the Business landing page first, followed by segment pages, product pages, and support pages. All tasks were broken into Jira stories delegated across designers, writers, and developers.

Every sprint was 2 weeks long with 4 working sessions each. We delivered solutions backed with data and user research every session, receiving feedback which we incorporated iteratively. This balanced what the client wanted with business goals and user needs.

Research Findings

Relationships matter -especially the ones you haven't formed yet

While on this engagement, our team had a lot of quick wins. We hit the nail on the head early on in many sprints, saving a lot of iteration work -thanks to proper research and a great client team. Our team created a lot of noise, earning praise across Brightspeed and internally at CGI, building trust between the two companies.
