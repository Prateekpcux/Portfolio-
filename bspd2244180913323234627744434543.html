Brightspeed — Business Solutions

Redesigning the B2B digital experience for the nation's 5th largest telecom — improving navigation, trust, and conversion after a major post-acquisition brand transition.

Telecom · Enterprise B2B Experience Redesign CGI Inc. 150+ Pages
Background

The nation's 5th largest telecom, newly independent

Brightspeed is valued at over $7.5 billion USD and is expanding its fiber optic network to reach 1 million new connections. As a spin-off of Lumen and CenturyLink, it serves 6.5 million locations in rural and suburban areas across the southeast, southwest, and great lakes regions.

The Problem

Post-acquisition brand and UX fragmentation

In November 2021, Apollo Global Management acquired Brightspeed from Lumen Technologies. The website felt disjointed due to lingering Lumen and CenturyLink branding, subpar navigation, and degraded support experiences. Existing users dropped off, causing significant client attrition.

The Solution

Cohesive B2B experience with clear navigation

A full experience redesign of Brightspeed Business Solutions — replacing legacy navigation, rebuilding the information architecture, and delivering a consistent brand experience aligned with Brightspeed's new identity.

Design Work

From legacy pages to a unified B2B experience

Working at CGI, I led the migration of 150+ webpages and the redesign of the business solutions experience. The work covered information architecture, navigation redesign, component library alignment, and high-fidelity page designs.
