Experience Overview

I'm a Senior UX Designer with 8+ years of experience helping clients across federal government, healthcare, telecom, automotive, and enterprise analytics industries. I specialize in turning complex systems into intuitive, trusted digital products -from early discovery and information architecture through high-fidelity UI and governance workflows.

My Story

I began in 2015 with a Bachelor's in Computer Science from Mumbai University, then relocated to the US for a Master's in Interactive Media & Game Development at WPI, specializing in HCI. During grad school I joined Carmaguys, a startup for automotive services, handling both UX and front-end development. After graduating, I joined Softrams as a full-stack UX/UI designer before moving to enterprise healthcare at Inova Health System.

Inova Health System

Currently I lead UX at Inova in the Washington DC–Virginia area, designing enterprise analytics products, AI governance experiences, clinical dashboard systems, and the Periscope analytics marketplace -bridging governance, data, and clinical decision-making into trusted digital products.

CGI Inc. -Telecom

Led migration of 150+ webpages from Lumen and CenturyLink for a multi-billion dollar telecom. The redesigned experience achieved a 47% surge in page views, 45% increase in unique pageviews, and 70% reduction in bounce rate. Received the 'Partnership and Quality' value pin for delivery excellence.

Softrams -Federal Healthcare

Led UX research and design for ACO-MS, a federal CMS healthcare product, and HDrive, a healthcare SaaS platform. ACO-MS users saved $1.2 billion in Medicare in 2019, up from $739M the prior year. By 2022, 66 new enrollments, 140 renewals, and over 11 million beneficiaries received care -a 3% growth.
