The Problem

Discoverability was the symptom.
Trust was the disease.

On the surface, clinicians couldn't find analytics assets. Dig deeper — even when they found something, they emailed the analytics team to ask "is this current?" before using it. The real design problem was making trust legible at browse time.

● Before
Fragmented. Six tools, no shared taxonomy, no trust signals. Users needed 4+ bookmarks to find one dashboard.
● After
Periscope. One entry point. Certification visible before you click. Every asset searchable, governed, and trusted.
What was broken
  • Power BI, Tableau, Signal 1, Vizient, Epic, SharePoint — no shared taxonomy
  • Duplicate reports everywhere — no one knew which version was official
  • No certification visibility — users emailed analytics team before trusting any asset
  • AI models with no governance, oversight, or expiry tracking
  • New employees had no onboarding path into analytics whatsoever
Stale data decisionsDuplicate workZero AI oversight
The mandate
  • Unified search + browse across all six platforms and asset types
  • Certification state visible on the card — not buried in metadata after clicking
  • Dedicated AI model inventory with performance, expiry, and approval chain
  • Campaigns path for clinical users who think in outcomes, not data taxonomies
  • Governance workflows for admins — certify, deprecate, manage asset lifecycle

Critical Decisions

Three calls that shaped the platform.

Research told us what mattered. These three decisions are how we resolved the tensions — each one a call a junior designer wouldn't have made.

01
Governance visible at card level —
not the detail page

Early prototypes buried certification status in a metadata tab. Users scrolled past it every single time — they'd already formed an opinion about the asset from the card. By the time they reached the detail page, the trust signal was too late.

  • Early version: certification in metadata tab on detail page
  • Discovery: users made trust decisions at the card, not after clicking
  • Fix: elevated status badge, owner, last validated date to the card surface
→ Trust at browse time · no more "email to verify"
✓ Certified — visible here

Browse grid — certification badge on every card before clicking

02
Campaigns — a parallel browse path
for clinical users

The analytics team wanted a pure taxonomy browse. Clinical managers wanted "show me everything for my HIMSS Stage 7 audit." These are incompatible at the top level — taxonomy requires knowing tool names; clinical users think in outcomes and initiatives.

  • Taxonomy browse failed clinical users in every session — they don't think in tool types
  • Clinical managers think in: HIMSS Stage 7, Cost Reduction, Heart Failure — not Power BI vs Tableau
  • Fix: Campaigns as a parallel entry point, not a category within taxonomy
→ 2× faster for clinical users · no tool-type knowledge required
Initiative-based, not tool-based

Campaigns — clinical users browse by initiative, bypassing taxonomy entirely

03
AI models as first-class assets —
not a filter option

The initial scope treated AI models as just another asset type within the browse filter. But AI carries oversight obligations no other asset has — performance monitoring, certification expiry, bias review, approval chains. Treating it as a filter made those obligations invisible.

  • Initial scope: AI = one checkbox in the asset type filter
  • AI requires performance oversight, expiry tracking, and bias review — no other asset type does
  • Fix: dedicated AI Inventory with its own governance workflow — became the most-discussed feature with leadership
→ Inova's first dedicated AI governance experience

Design System

Built on Inova's foundation.
Extended for governance.

Periscope was built entirely within Inova's enterprise system — Arial typeface, semantic color tokens, established components. My job wasn't to invent new styles. It was to extend the system with four patterns the marketplace needed that didn't exist yet.

New patterns added to the system

Asset Cards

Trust-first tile — cert badge, tool icon, owner, last validated visible before clicking

AI Model Cards

Performance state, expiry countdown, bias flag — no equivalent in the base system

Governance Chips

6-state certification mapped to Inova semantic tokens

Campaign Tiles

Initiative-grouping pattern with clinical pathway hero treatment

Six certification states
Certified
Green badge on card surface — visible before clicking
In Review
Governance workflow currently in progress
Expired / Flagged
Surfaced at browse time — not hidden after click
Deprecated
Grey — shown in search to redirect, never hidden

Periscope moved Inova from fragmented report hunting to a governed, searchable, trust-aware marketplace. The real design challenge wasn't making assets easier to find — it was making every user feel confident that what they found was certified and appropriate for clinical decision-making.
