BACKGROUND

Periscope is an enterprise analytics marketplace designed to provide a one-stop shop for discovering, accessing, and understanding analytics assets across Inova Health System. Prior to Periscope, dashboards, reports, AI models, extracts, and documentation were distributed across multiple platforms including Power BI, Tableau, Epic, SharePoint, AI platforms, Excel sheet, Strata Reports and SlicerDicer.

As analytics adoption grew, users often struggled to locate trusted resources, identify ownership, and determine which assets should be used for decision-making. The goal was to create a centralized experience that improved discoverability, transparency, governance, and adoption while reducing duplicate work across teams.

The goal was to create a centralized experience that improved:
• Discoverability
• Transparency
• Governance
• Adoption
• Operational Efficiency

THE PROBLEM

Analytics resources were scattered across Power BI, Power BI, Tableau, Epic, SharePoint, AI platforms, Excel sheet, Strata Reports and SlicerDicer, making them difficult to discover and manage.

Challenges users faced:
• Finding trusted assets
• Understanding ownership
• Identifying certified resources for decision-making
• Avoiding duplicate work across teams

THE SOLUTION

We designed a centralized marketplace experience that provides an efficient, Amazon-like way for users to search, preview, launch, organize, and understand analytics assets across the organization.


The platform combined powerful search, filters, metadata-driven discovery, asset details, ownership visibility, certification status, favorites, collections, and a dedicated AI inventory experience to make dashboards, reports, AI models, and data assets easier to find, trust, and reuse.

DISCOVERY & KEY INSIGHTS
Through stakeholder workshops and working sessions, we identified common patterns in how teams searched for dashboards, validated ownership, and determined whether resources were safe to use.

Ownership was unclear

Users needed to know who owned, supported, and maintained each asset.

Trust signals mattered

Certification, status, and governance labels needed to be visible.

Reuse needed support

Favorites and related assets helped reduce duplicate work.

DESIGN DIRECTION
Search-first homepage
Search was elevated as the main interaction because users often knew what they needed, but did not know where it lived. The homepage was designed to reduce friction and immediately guide users into discovery.
