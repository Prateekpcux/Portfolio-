PERISCOPE
BACKGROUND

Periscope is an enterprise analytics marketplace created to help Inova users discover, access, and trust analytics assets in one place.

Before Periscope, dashboards, reports, AI models, extracts, documentation, and operational insights were spread across Power BI, Tableau, Epic, SharePoint, Signal 1, and internal repositories.

The goal was to create an Amazon-like discovery experience that improved findability, governance, transparency, and adoption across Inova Health System.

THE PROBLEM

Analytics assets were fragmented across multiple systems, making discovery slow, inconsistent, and difficult to trust.

  • Users struggled to find trusted dashboards, reports, and AI models.
  • Ownership, certification, and access paths were often unclear.
  • Teams recreated similar reports instead of reusing existing assets.
  • There was limited preview context before launching or requesting access.
THE SOLUTION

We designed Periscope as a centralized single source of truth for analytics assets, AI models, KPIs, and initiatives.

  • Unified search and filters to quickly discover relevant assets.
  • Asset detail pages with previews, owners, documentation, and access paths.
  • Governance indicators for certification, metadata, and lifecycle status.
  • Campaigns grouped assets around priorities like Cost Reduction, Quality Improvement, and HIMSS Stage 7.

The final experience helps users search, preview, trust, save, and launch assets with confidence.
