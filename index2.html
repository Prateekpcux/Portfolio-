BACKGROUND
Periscope is an enterprise analytics marketplace designed to help Inova teams discover trusted dashboards, reports, extracts, AI models, and analytics resources across multiple systems. The goal was to create a single experience where users could search, evaluate, understand, and request access to analytics assets with greater confidence.
THE PROBLEM
Analytics resources were scattered across Power BI, Tableau, Epic, SharePoint, extracts, and AI platforms. Users struggled to know which assets were trusted, who owned them, and whether the information was certified for decision-making.
THE SOLUTION
We designed a centralized marketplace experience with search, filters, asset details, certification visibility, ownership, favorites, AI inventory, and metadata-driven discovery to make analytics easier to find, trust, and reuse.
MY ROLE & ROADMAP
I worked as the Senior UX/UI Designer, partnering with Data Governance, Analytics, AI, engineering, and clinical stakeholders. My responsibilities included UX strategy, stakeholder workshops, information architecture, metadata strategy, wireframes, high-fidelity design, and iterative design reviews.
  • Define the discovery experience for enterprise analytics assets.
  • Create scalable navigation, metadata, and filtering patterns.
  • Design marketplace pages including homepage, browse, asset details, favorites, campaigns, and AI inventory.
  • Support engineering with design handoff and product decisions.

DISCOVERY & KEY INSIGHTS
Through stakeholder workshops and working sessions, we identified common patterns in how teams searched for dashboards, validated ownership, and determined whether resources were safe to use.

Ownership was unclear

Users needed to know who owned, supported, and maintained each asset.

Trust signals mattered

Certification, status, and governance labels needed to be visible.

Reuse needed support

Favorites and related assets helped reduce duplicate work.

DESIGN DIRECTION
Search-first homepage
Search was elevated as the main interaction because users often knew what they needed, but did not know where it lived. The homepage was designed to reduce friction and immediately guide users into discovery.
INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE & METADATA
One of the most important parts of the product was creating a scalable structure for thousands of analytics resources. We organized assets using consistent categories and metadata fields.
