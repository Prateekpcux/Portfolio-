The Problem

Healthcare data is fragmented and incomplete

As populations grow and age, holistic health management becomes increasingly important. Disparate systems track different pieces of the health equation — but none provide the whole-person, longitudinal perspective people need to make informed decisions.

The Solution

Healthcare data in a briefcase

HDrive brings together citizens, providers, physician groups, and hospitals to securely store and exchange health-related data — including EHR, encounter data, claims, and quality metrics. Combined with wearable data and AI, it delivers holistic healthcare insights for every individual.

My Role

Sole designer and researcher for both Alpha and Beta phases — running usability tests and designing the application from the ground up.

Tools

Sketch, InVision, Optimal Workshop, Miro, Jira, Google Docs, WebAIM (508 testing), HTML/Bootstrap

Users

Patients, providers, physician groups, hospitals, and insurance companies — each with distinct access needs and data views.

Research & Validation Process

From stakeholder goals to shipped product

Step 1

Stakeholder Research

Gathered data from internal stakeholders and PM to understand client needs and business goals.

Step 2

Competitive Analysis

Explored similar apps via competitive and SWOT analysis; presented findings to PM and CTO.

Step 3

User Personas & IA

Created personas, then used card sorting and user surveys to define information architecture.

Step 4

Wireframes & Testing

Whiteboard wireframes → low-fidelity prototypes → usability tests with 5 users per round.

Design System & High-Fidelity

Building a scalable visual foundation

After usability testing validated the interaction model, I built a full design system for high-fidelity prototypes — covering components, typography, color, and layout patterns for both provider and patient views.

Platform Overview

Core architecture and data model.

HDrive was designed with 508 accessibility compliance from day one — validated using WebAIM tools throughout both Alpha and Beta phases. The application was built on HTML/Bootstrap with InVision prototypes driving developer handoff.
