UX Case StudyHDrive · Jan 2021 – Jul 2021

HDrive Marketplace

An Android/iOS app for checking drug coverage from a plan ID or ZIP code — designed as sole UX designer through qualitative research and user flow design.

Mobile UXHealthcare SaaSDrug CoverageQualitative Research
Background

Drug coverage discovery made simple

HDrive Marketplace is a mobile app that lets patients check whether specific drugs are covered under a given plan ID or ZIP code — reducing confusion around medication benefits and out-of-pocket costs.

My Role

Sole UX Designer

I led all qualitative UX research and design. The research team handled quantitative analysis. Together we moved from business goals through user flows to final deliverable.

Tools

Figma, Miro, Jira

Timeline

January 2021 – July 2021

Platform

Android & iOS (mobile app)

Qualitative Research

I analyzed similar drug coverage apps and collected competitive data to understand what worked and what didn't. The research team simultaneously defined user flows based on goals established by the product manager and CTO.

Final Deliverable

Interactive prototype

The final interactive prototype demonstrates the end-to-end coverage lookup experience across plan ID and ZIP code flows.

Outcome

Alpha phase complete, scaling planned

The product is currently in alpha phase, with mobile release planned alongside the broader HDrive platform. The design system and user flows are positioned to scale as plan coverage databases expand.

The Marketplace extends HDrive's vision of bringing healthcare data together for patients — adding medication coverage clarity to a platform that already unifies EHR, wearable, and claims data.
